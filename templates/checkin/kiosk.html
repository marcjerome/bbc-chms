To use the camera over http (Chrome or Edge, one-time setup on this device):

  1. Open chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure (Edge: edge://flags/…)
  2. Type in the box and set it to Enabled.
  3. Tap Relaunch, come back here and allow the camera.

iPhone/iPad Safari can't do this; use an Android tablet, a laptop, or a USB scanner.
