# Pitch positioning: BBC Barani ChMS

Market scan done 2026-11-22. The sources are vendor marketing pages; their wording was checked on the live pages only where marked ✔.

## 1. How the market pitches church software

| Vendor | How they lead | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Planning Center Check-Ins ✔ | "Keep children safe, give parents peace." Home page: "Every part of your church, finally in sync." | Check-in is sold as **safety and peace of mind**, not record-keeping |
| Tithely (formerly Breeze) ✔ | "Easy-to-use so everyone will use it"; "Simply Serve" | Sold on **simplicity for volunteers** |
| Pushpay ChMS | Strengthening connections; coordinating care for needs | Sold as **care coordination** |
| ChurchTrac blog ✔ | Absentee reports as "pursuing lost sheep", "re-establish contact" with those who "wandered from the fold" | Attendance data is **for relationship**, not for the record |
| Tithely blog | "Data-driven shepherding"; absence alerts route a task to a leader | Absence is a **trigger to reach out** |
| Brushfire (events) | "Focus on ministry while we manage logistics" | Conferences: **fewer lines, more ministry** |
| Subsplash / TouchPoint (events) | Fast "tap-and-go" check-in; every registration linked to the church database | Conference check-in sold on **speed** |

**The pattern:** nobody leads with "track attendance". The good ones lead with **people**: care, safety, connection, ministry time. Attendance is the quiet means, never the headline.

**In the Philippines:** I found only an academic study (Bulacan State U., 2026) of a church fingerprint attendance system, and no commercial product. Its authors say trust depends on **secure template storage and transparent data governance**, which our consent-first, templates-stay-on-the-device design already provides. That is a real differentiator locally.

## 2. Our positioning (proposed)

> **"So no one slips away unnoticed."** A ministry tool that frees ushers from the logbook and shows the pastor who needs a visit, a call or a prayer this week.

Core idea: **attendance is not the product. Visitation is.** The system notices so the church can *care*.

Scripture anchors (KJV):
- **Ephesians 5:16**: "Redeeming the time, because the days are evil." Less paperwork, more shepherding.
- **Proverbs 27:23**: "Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks."
- **Luke 15:4**: the shepherd who leaves the ninety and nine to go after the one.
- **1 Peter 5:2**: "Feed the flock of God… taking the oversight thereof, not by constraint, but willingly."

> Avoid Hebrews 10:25 ("not forsaking the assembling…") in the pitch. It's true, but it sounds like policing.

## 2b. The growth problem `[M]` (Marc, 2026-11-22)

**BBC Barani is growing.** When a church is small, the pastor knows everyone: who's sick, who's struggling, who hasn't been around. As attendance grows, that knowledge no longer fits in one person's head or one usher's logbook. People get lost **not because nobody cares, but because nobody can keep up**.

The system's value for the pastor and workers is to **keep the "small church" knowing in a growing church**:
- the pastor sees the state of the whole flock at a glance (trends, who we missed, new faces);
- workers and ushers share one memory of the church family instead of scattered notebooks;
- every new visitor is remembered and welcomed back, not just counted.

Biblical precedent, which fits a Baptist audience well:
- **Acts 6:1–3**: "when the number of the disciples was multiplied… their widows were neglected", so the church organized helpers so no one was overlooked. *Growth caused neglect; organization answered it.*
- **Exodus 18:17–18** (Jethro to Moses): "thou art not able to perform it thyself alone." Shepherding has to be shared.

Pitch line: **"A growing church shouldn't mean a lonelier church."**

Future idea `[P]`: assign members to **care groups / workers** (e.g. each deacon or worker shepherds a list of families), so the "We missed you" list goes straight to the person responsible. This would be the natural phase 3, mirroring Acts 6.

## 3. Words to use and words to avoid

| Instead of… | Say… |
|---|---|
| Attendance tracking / monitoring | Knowing our flock · caring for who's here, and who isn't |
| Absent / absentee list | **We missed you** · **Visitation list** |
| Not checked in | Not yet seen today |
| Biometric attendance | **Quick check-in**, a touch at the door |
| Compliance, monitor, track, record | Remember, notice, welcome, pray for |
| Data / analytics | Seeing the season of our church |

## 4. Value story by audience

- **Pastor:** "However big the church grows, you still know your flock. Every Monday you know who to visit, who to call and whose birthday to pray over, without asking around."
- **Ushers:** "Greet people instead of holding a logbook. A touch, or a tap on the phone, and you're done."
- **Members:** "If you're sick or struggling and miss church, someone notices and reaches out. Your fingerprint never leaves the device, and you choose whether to join."
- **Secretary:** "One clean list of the church family: households, birthdays, milestones."
- **Conferences and fellowships:** "Hundreds of delegates checked in quickly, with live headcounts per church and session, and no clipboard lines." (See section 6.)

## 5. Pitch video storyline (maps to `pitch-demo.md`)

1. **Open on the problem (heart, not software):** the church is growing, praise God, but the pastor can't know everyone's state anymore (Acts 6:1). A Sunday logbook, ushers busy writing, and a member who has quietly stopped coming. Nobody noticed for weeks.
2. **The turn:** "What if the church could notice, so we can care?" Ephesians 5:16.
3. **Sunday morning:** a touch at the door (simulator), and on the usher's phone people appear live. The usher is free to greet.
4. **Sunday announcements:** the Birthdays this week list, printed and prayed over.
5. **Monday:** the pastor opens **Visitation**: who we missed, and first-time visitors to welcome. One tap calls them.
6. **Trust:** consent first (RA 10173), and fingerprints stay on the device. "Your choice, your privacy."
7. **Close:** "Less time on records. More time with people." Then the church name and the verse.

## 6. Conferences (the user's idea): fit and gaps

What works today: services can be one-off events (custom title, date and time), guests are counted, first-time visitors are added at the door, and biometric scans auto-match the session that's running.

Gaps for a real multi-church conference `[P]`, not built:
- delegates from **other churches** (the "home church" of each guest);
- **pre-registration** before the event, with a QR code or name badge;
- per-session and per-church **headcount reports**;
- several check-in points at once (this already works, but is untested at scale).

Suggestion: show conferences in the pitch as a **"coming next"** slide, not in the live demo.

## 7. UI wording changes to match this framing ([M], applied 2026-11-22)

- Sidebar "Follow-ups" → **"Visitation"** ✅ done
- "Absent last Sunday Worship" → **"We missed you last Sunday Worship"**
- "Not checked in (N)" → **"Not yet seen (N)"**
- Biometrics menu → **"Quick check-in"** (the data-privacy page keeps the word "biometric" for legal clarity)
