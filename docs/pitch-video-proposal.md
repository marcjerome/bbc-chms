---

## 1. The values, through Baptist distinctives

The system exists to serve the Bible's pattern for a local New Testament church. Each feature is framed by the distinctive it serves, not by what the software does.

| Baptist distinctive | What the system serves | Verse (KJV) |
|---|---|---|
| **Biblical authority** | We don't adopt a worldly "management" model. The Bible already tells the shepherd to know the flock | *"Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, and look well to thy herds."* (Proverbs 27:23) |
| **Autonomy of the local church** | The system is **owned and run by BBC Barani**: our server, our records. No outside board, no foreign subscription, no one else deciding for us | *"And he is the head of the body, the church…"* (Colossians 1:18) |
| **Priesthood of every believer** | Ministry isn't only the pastor's. Ushers, workers and deacons share one record of the church family, and each does their part | *"For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ."* (Ephesians 4:12) |
| **Two ordinances** | Salvation, believer's baptism and membership are recorded as milestones of each person's walk, in the Bible's order | *"Then they that gladly received his word were baptized: and the same day there were added unto them about three thousand souls."* (Acts 2:41) |
| **Individual soul liberty** | No one is compelled. Quick check-in (fingerprint) is **by personal consent only**, and it can be withdrawn anytime | *"Let every man be fully persuaded in his own mind."* (Romans 14:5) |
| **Saved, baptized church membership** | A clear, honest church roll: who are members, who are regular attendees, who are first-time visitors still hearing the Gospel | *"And the Lord added to the church daily such as should be saved."* (Acts 2:47) |
| **Two offices: pastor and deacons** | The **pastor** sees the state of the flock and **deacons** help bear the care (phase 3: care groups) | *"Take heed therefore unto yourselves, and to all the flock…"* (Acts 20:28) · *"…look ye out among you seven men… whom we may appoint over this business."* (Acts 6:3) |
| **Separation of church and state** | The church governs itself, and still honours the law of the land (Data Privacy Act, RA 10173) in how it keeps personal records | *"Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's."* (Matthew 22:21) |

**Our central conviction:** *"I am the good shepherd, and know my sheep, and am known of mine."* (John 10:14). The Lord knows His own by name. As the church grows, we want to keep knowing one another the same way.

## 2. The pitch (60–90 seconds, spoken or on a one-pager)

> *"He calleth his own sheep by name."* (John 10:3)
>
> By God's grace, BBC Barani is growing. Souls are being saved, baptized and added to the church. But as the Lord adds to our number, our care has to keep up. A brother is sick for a few Sundays; a sister goes through a hard season; a first-time visitor heard the Gospel and is waiting to be welcomed back. We love them, but as we grow, **we don't always find out in time.**
>
> The church in Jerusalem faced this too. *"When the number of the disciples was multiplied… their widows were neglected in the daily ministration."* (Acts 6:1). They didn't stop growing; they organized so that no one was overlooked.
>
> **The BBC Barani Church Management System** helps us do the same, decently and in order. Every Sunday we know whose birthdays to pray for. Every week the pastor and workers see who we missed, so we can go *"house to house"* (Acts 20:20) and visit them.
>
> At the door, a simple **touch of a finger** checks each person in and greets them by name, so our ushers are free to welcome people instead of writing in logbooks. It belongs to our church, and it respects every member's liberty: no one is enrolled without their own consent, and the fingerprint never leaves the terminal.
>
> **A growing church shouldn't mean a lonelier church.** *"Redeeming the time, because the days are evil."* (Ephesians 5:16)

**One sentence:** *It helps our growing church keep knowing one another by name, so the pastor and workers can visit, pray for and care for every soul, decently and in order.*

**Audience:** the pastor, deacons and workers of BBC Barani.
**The ask:** approval to start using it for four Sundays (the MVP), and to buy one fingerprint/face terminal for the main door (about ₱5–15k, to confirm with suppliers).

## 3. Wording rules for the video

- Talk about **us noticing and caring**, never about members leaving. Don't use "slip away", "wander", "lost sheep", "track" or "monitor".
- **Visitation** is the heart of it, a word Baptists already love (Thursday visitation, house-to-house).
- Present people as **souls and families**, not records or data.
- Present technology as a **servant** of the ministry, "decently and in order" (1 Corinthians 14:40).
